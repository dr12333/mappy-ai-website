Mappy for Schools: help students see the bigger picture.

Mappy is a digital mind-mapping workspace for schools. Teachers and students can structure ideas together in class, while students can continue learning individually for revision and exam prep.

Start free 30-day pilot Contact us
Pilot includes up to 3 teachers and 150 students. No long-term commitment required.

Why schools choose Mappy

Keep the learning value of mind mapping, but make it faster to build, easier to update, and practical for collaborative classrooms.

Built for class collaboration

  • Create shared maps live during lessons.
  • Use group work to develop topics visually.
  • Keep one structure everyone can build on.

Strong for individual learning

  • Students can review lessons in their own map flow.
  • Organize key ideas before tests and assessments.
  • Understand connections instead of memorizing isolated facts.
Why it beats paper maps
  • Edit quickly without starting from scratch.
  • Expand maps over time as topics grow.
  • Support collaborative and individual use in one tool.

Common school use cases

From first explanation to final exam prep, Mappy supports both teaching flow and student learning flow.

Brainstorming in class

Capture ideas in real time and build a shared visual structure together.

Structuring new topics

Create a clear overview of concepts, subtopics, and relationships.

Group projects

Let students co-create maps for research, presentations, and discussion.

Revision and exam prep

Help students summarize and revisit content in a structured visual format.

Independent study support

Give students a practical way to organize what they learned on their own.

Learning to work with AI

Students build AI literacy in practice. Teachers can enable or disable any features when needed.

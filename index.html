What makes Mappy AI different

Things you won't find in another mindmap tool.

Real images, not AI illustrations

Most mindmap tools don't embed images at all. The ones that include them rely on generic AI illustrations. Mappy AI pulls the actual figures from your PDF — or real photos from the web for abstract topics. Real visuals tied to real sources.

Sources embedded, not footnoted

In Visual mode, nodes that reference a diagram or figure carry a snapshot of the source PDF page. Click any node, choose "View source page" from its menu, and the original PDF opens at the exact page that informed it. Sources stay one click away — not buried behind a citation.

AI scoped to a branch

Click any node to open its menu, then choose an AI action — the AI works on just that branch, not the whole map. Faster, more accurate, and less prone to hallucination than the chat-sidebar pattern other tools use.

@mention any node by name

Type @ in chat to reference any node precisely. No more "the third bullet under chemistry" — Mappy AI knows exactly which node you mean.

AI that respects your edits

When the AI updates your map, it plans the steps first, then runs each one through a scoped tool — add a child here, rename a label there. Your manual edits survive instead of getting wiped by a regeneration.

From audio to mindmap in one step

Drop in a lecture recording, voice memo, or any audio file. Mappy AI transcribes it, structures the content into topics, and adds related images. A 60-minute lecture becomes a 2-minute scan.

FAQ

Everything you need to know before you start.

What is included in the free plan?

Free includes up to 3 mindmaps, 5 AI generations (total), manual editing tools, and cloud sync. No subscription needed to get started. It does not include collaboration or PDF export.

What happens when I reach the 3-mindmap limit?

You can still open, edit, and delete your existing mindmaps. To create more, upgrade to Pro or Max for unlimited mindmaps.

What is the difference between Pro and Max?

Pro adds unlimited mindmaps, 40 AI generations per month, and PDF export. Max includes everything in Pro plus collaborative mindmapping, priority support, and 300 AI generations per month.

How many AI generations do I get?

Free includes 5 AI generations total. Pro includes 40 generations per month. Max includes 300 generations per month. Chat and explain requests don't count — only mindmap generations, refines, and image attaches.

How does yearly billing work?

The yearly price is shown as an effective monthly rate. Yearly billing gives a 25% discount compared with monthly billing.

What happens if I cancel?

Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period. Your maps are never deleted.

How are my files used for AI?

Files and links are used to ground your AI requests. We do not sell or share your data.

What file types can I upload?

You can add PDFs, Word documents, slides, spreadsheets, images, audio, videos, and links to help Mappy AI ground its responses.

Can I export my mindmaps?

Export to PDF is available on Pro and Max plans.

Is my data private?

Your content stays in your workspace and is only used to answer your requests.

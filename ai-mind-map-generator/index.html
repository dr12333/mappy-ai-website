How it works

Three steps from source material to a usable map.

1. Add your source

Upload a PDF, drop in a lecture recording, paste a YouTube link, or share any URL — Mappy works with all of them.

2. Let Mappy AI build the structure

It turns the content into a clean hierarchy you can scan immediately.

3. Deepen the branches that matter

Ask follow-up questions, expand specific sections, and export when you are ready.

What you get

Grounded generation

The generated map stays tied to the material you uploaded instead of floating as generic AI output.

Targeted expansion

Grow one branch at a time without losing the structure of the rest of your map.

Visual mind maps with images

Switch to Visual mode and Mappy attaches images to your nodes — page snapshots from PDFs or topic images from the web — so the generated map looks like a textbook, not a wall of bullets.

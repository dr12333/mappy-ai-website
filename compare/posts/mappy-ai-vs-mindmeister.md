## FAQ
**Is MindMeister better for teams than Mappy AI?**
For large teams that need admin controls, SAML SSO, compliance exports, and deep integrations with tools like Google Workspace and Microsoft Teams, MindMeister's Business plan is stronger. Mappy AI offers collaboration on its Max plan, which works well for small teams and study groups but does not include enterprise governance features.

**Does MindMeister include AI on all plans?**
MindMeister offers AI map creation in beta across all plans, including the free tier. However, AI is a supplementary feature alongside traditional manual mapping. In Mappy AI, AI-powered generation is the core workflow — every plan includes AI features, and the paid tiers offer significantly more AI generations per month.

**Which tool is better for individual exam revision?**
Mappy AI is designed specifically for source-grounded study. You upload lecture slides, PDFs, or links and get structured mind maps that trace back to your materials. MindMeister is a general-purpose mapping tool that works well for note-taking and brainstorming but does not offer the same source-ingestion workflow.

**Why does MindMeister pricing vary by country?**
MindMeister uses locale-rendered pricing, meaning the displayed price and currency change based on your region. For example, European users see EUR pricing while Swiss users see CHF. Always check [mindmeister.com/pricing](https://www.mindmeister.com/en/pricing) for your local rates before subscribing.

**Can I export my mind maps from both tools?**
Both tools support PDF export on paid plans. MindMeister additionally offers PNG, JPG, and Microsoft Office format exports on higher tiers. Mappy AI currently focuses on PDF export. If you need image or Office format exports, MindMeister has more options.

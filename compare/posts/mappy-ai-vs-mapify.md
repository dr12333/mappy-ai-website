## FAQ
**Is Mappy AI cheaper than Mapify for students?**
Yes. Mappy AI's paid plan starts at $4.50/month (yearly), compared to Mapify's Basic at $5.99/month (yearly). Mappy AI also has a free tier with 3 mindmaps and 5 AI generations, while Mapify's free tier offers 30 one-time credits. For ongoing student use, Mappy AI's fixed pricing is more predictable than Mapify's credit-based model.

**Does Mapify support more export formats than Mappy AI?**
Yes. Mapify supports PDF, Markdown, and SVG exports, plus slide-style presentations. Mappy AI currently supports PDF export on paid plans. If you need Markdown or SVG output, Mapify has the edge here.

**Which tool is better for study workflows using class materials?**
Mappy AI is purpose-built for source-grounded study. You upload PDFs, slides, or links and get mind maps that trace back to your materials. Mapify handles similar inputs but optimizes for broad summarization rather than source traceability.

**Can I collaborate on mind maps with both tools?**
Mappy AI includes collaboration on its Max plan ($15/mo monthly). Mapify offers team features through a Contact Sales path rather than self-serve collaboration tiers. For straightforward team collaboration, Mappy AI's upgrade path is simpler.

**Does Mapify have a mobile app?**
Yes. Mapify has native apps for iOS, Android, iPadOS, and macOS, plus a browser extension. Mappy AI is currently web-first, accessible on any device through the browser but without dedicated native apps.
